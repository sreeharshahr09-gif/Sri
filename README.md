## [ResumeAgent](ResumeAgent/) — local résumé & cover-letter tailoring agent

Paste a job description and your CV. A local llama.cpp model (e.g. Qwen) then rewrites your
résumé and cover letter in a fact-checked loop. It pushes the **truthful** JD match toward 100%
and never invents skills. Exports Word, PDF, TXT and ZIP. Everything runs on your own PC.

Start with [`ResumeAgent/START_HERE.txt`](ResumeAgent/START_HERE.txt) (Windows: double-click `start_windows.bat`).
