Start here. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
  1. Load a tread plan. Load DXF… for your own drawing, or Load sample to try the bundled 130/80R17 plan.
  2. Set what the drawing cannot tell you — non-skid depth, draft angle, sipes, compound hardness. A 2D plan carries no depth.
  3. Describe the contact patch — pick a shape and type its size, or drag the handles on the preview.
  4. Press Run. The sweep takes a couple of seconds in a background worker; every chart then updates in place.
