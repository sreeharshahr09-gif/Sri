Section 02

Rolling Resistance — Deep Dive

1,150 patents over 2015–2024 address rolling resistance. The technical framing has shifted: from chemistry-first (silica + silane) toward a thermal systems approach where compound, construction, and geometry are treated together. Heat/hysteresis language now accounts for more patents than direct RR terminology — reflecting how compound scientists frame the problem at the lab level.

RR Patent Keyword Group Trends (2015–2024)
Heat/Hysteresis language and Fuel Consumption now together represent more filings than direct "rolling resistance" terminology. Belt/cord enters RR framing from 2019 onward.
Key Finding

Heat / Hysteresis Language Overtakes Direct RR

Patents using loss tangent, tan delta, and heat build-up grew from 32 (2015) to 66 (2023). Japanese and Korean OEMs write compound-science patents — they describe the same RR objective through measurement terms, not application terms.

Key Finding

Fuel Consumption — China's Dominant Vocabulary

"Fuel consumption reduction" is the primary RR framing for Chinese filers. At 17 (2015) → 51 (2018) → 42 (2024), this group captures a fleet-commercial perspective entirely missed by narrow RR keyword searches.

Emerging Signal

Belt/Cord Entering the RR Patent Space

Belt and cord structure mentions within RR patents grew from 8 (2015) to 29 (2023) and 27 (2024). Engineers increasingly treat construction — not just rubber chemistry — as an RR lever. A newer framing with room to grow.

Heat/Hysteresis is now the #1 theme. Fuel Consumption nearly equal. Both exceed direct silica and carbon black counts.

China Signal

Volume + Vocabulary Shift

Chinese RR filings tripled from 12/yr (2015) to 60/yr (2023). Vocabulary also shifted — from tread pattern geometry to rubber compound formulation — signalling a technical capability upgrade.

Japan Signal

Volume Declining, Quality Held

Japanese RR filings fell from 53 (2015) to 32 (2024). Major OEMs appear to be shifting core compound innovations to trade secrets. Physics-term vocabulary (loss tangent, tan delta) remains their signature.

Germany Signal

Continental Re-engaging on RR

Continental filed 12 German RR patents in 2024 — its highest ever single year. Including a unique patent combining RR optimisation with EV electrical conductivity — the only patent of its kind in the dataset.

Section 03

Mileage & Tread Wear — Deep Dive

With 1,760 patents, mileage/wear is 53% larger than the RR pool. The approaches are structurally different: mileage is dominated by belt/cord construction and abrasion compound science, while heat management — despite being a primary driver of rubber degradation — remains underrepresented. Two equal peaks in 2019 and 2023 reflect separate drivers: fuel economy regulation and EU tire labelling pressure respectively.

Mileage Patent Keyword Group Trends (2015–2024)
Direct wear/abrasion terms and durability/life terms move in parallel. Tread pattern/depth is the fastest growing group 2020–2024. Retread is small but steadily growing.
New Finding

Retread: 88 Patents, Largely Ignored by Major OEMs

Yokohama (10) and Continental (9) lead retread patent activity. Sumitomo, Michelin, and Goodyear are near-absent despite retread being a primary TBR fleet value lever — extending tire life by 40–50% at fraction of new tire cost.

Accelerating Trend

Tread Pattern as a Wear Management Tool

Tread pattern/depth patents for mileage grew from 4/year (2015–2018) to 16–18/year (2022–2024). Groove geometry, block stiffness, and contact pressure distribution are now being patented as wear control levers, not just design features.

Framing Gap

Heat Management Severely Underweighted for Mileage

Heat management accounts for 31% of RR patents but only 9% of mileage patents. Thermal degradation of rubber is a primary driver of tread wear — yet this mechanism is rarely framed as a mileage innovation. A significant cross-domain blind spot.

Technology Sub-themes — Mileage Patents (n=1,760)
Abrasion/wear compound dominates at 48%. Belt/cord is #2. Contrast with RR (chemistry-first) is stark.

China overtook Japan in mileage patents from 2020–2021. Japan's 2024 count collapsed (22) — likely publication lag but also reflects portfolio maturation.
China Signal

China Now Leads Mileage Volume

China's mileage filings grew from 28/yr (2015) to 97/yr (2023), overtaking Japan from 2020. Chinese filings have also upgraded in scope — from tread pattern geometry to rubber compound formulations.

Japan Signal

Japan Retreating on Mileage Volume

Japan peaked at 94 mileage patents (2018) and dropped to 22 in 2024. Japanese OEMs are consolidating around fewer, higher-quality compound science patents rather than broad portfolio filings.

Korea Signal

Korea Closing the Gap

Hankook and Kumho together file a growing share of mileage patents with increasingly sophisticated compound chemistry — polyisobutylene-modified compounds, loss tangent-optimised tread formulations. Korea's share is growing year-on-year.

Section 06

Opportunity Areas & White Spaces

The following areas represent the clearest gaps between where industry is investing and where future value will be created — based on patent density, filing trajectory, and underlying technical logic. With the comprehensive keyword framework applied, these white spaces are confirmed: the low IP density is not an artefact of search limitations.

Opportunity Prioritisation — IP Density vs. Commercial Urgency
Bottom-right quadrant = low existing IP + high urgency = most attractive R&D targets. Grey bubbles = mature / crowded areas. Coloured bubbles = white space opportunities.
1

Thermal System Optimisation for RR + Mileage

Heat is the root cause of both energy dissipation (RR) and rubber degradation (wear). No patent treats it as a unified system — compound + belt + geometry co-optimised for thermal equilibrium. Heat management is 31% of RR patents but only 9% of mileage patents despite being the mechanism connecting both.

No System-Level PatentsHigh Commercial Pull
2

EV-Specific TBR: RR + Conductivity + Heavy Load + Wear

Continental's 2024 patent is the only one addressing the EV-specific conflict: silica-based low-RR compounds have low conductivity, yet EVs need static charge dissipation. Add heavier loads (batteries) and higher torque (faster wear) and there is no comprehensive EV-TBR system patent anywhere.

1 Patent WorldwideGrowing Market Urgency
3

AI/ML-Guided Compound Design for Dual RR + Mileage

Zero dual RR+Mileage patents use AI or simulation. The "magic triangle" (RR vs. wet grip vs. wear) is the classic multi-objective problem. ML-guided formulation using field wear data and lab tan-delta measurements to simultaneously optimise all three is technically feasible today and entirely unpatented at the system level.

0 Dual Patents on AIFirst-Mover Available
4

Retread-Optimised Compound Design (Multi-Life TBR)

88 retread patents, but only 8 address both RR and mileage. The multi-life compound problem differs from single-life: wear uniformity in first life, carcass adhesion preservation, and consistent compound properties for tread bonding in retread. No patent claims this integrated framework. Yokohama and Continental are active but narrow.

8 Dual Patents OnlyFleet TCO Impact
5

Contact Patch Pressure Homogeneity as an RR Lever

Tread pattern patents for RR grew from zero to 27 (2015–2024), mostly from Chinese OEMs using empirical groove geometry. The precise approach — computational contact mechanics to design block geometry that minimises peak pressure gradients, reducing deformation energy — has no patent coverage.

Sparse IP LandscapeDifferentiable Claim
6

Graphene as Network Modifier in Carbon Black Hybrid Systems

Graphene as a standalone filler peaked in 2021 and collapsed. A 2023 Chinese university patent showed graphene + multi-grade carbon black synergy during vulcanisation — improved service life and reduced hysteresis through network densification, not bulk filling. This bypasses the dispersion problem and is almost entirely unclaimed by industry.

1–2 PatentsHigh Technical Upside
7

Low-VOC + Low-RR Tread Compound (Dual Compliance)

One patent (China, 2025) targets low-VOC all-steel truck tread rubber. EU vehicle cabin air quality and tire sustainability regulations are converging. A tread compound achieving both low RR and low VOC emission addresses two regulatory pressures simultaneously — and the IP window is wide open.

1 Patent OnlyPre-Regulatory Window
8

Belt Cord Design Linking RR and Fatigue Life Together

Belt/cord patents appear in 15% of RR patents and 19% of mileage patents — but almost never as deliberate co-optimisation. Belt flexibility affects both rolling energy loss and belt edge separation fatigue. A broad system claim linking cord geometry to simultaneous RR and durability targets is an open territory.
