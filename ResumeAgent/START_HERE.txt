LOCAL RESUME + COVER LETTER AGENT V3
Windows / Anaconda / your existing llama.cpp server

1. Start your EXISTING llama-server with Qwen loaded (the one your Research Agent uses).
   Check in a browser: http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1/models   (port 8090 is also detected)

2. Double-click start_windows.bat in this folder.
   - It reuses C:\ProgramData\anaconda3\python.exe if it has the packages,
     otherwise it creates a private .venv and installs requirements.txt (first run only).
   - It never starts a second copy of the model.

3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8502 (or 8503 if 8502 is busy). Your Research Agent stays on 8501.

4. Sidebar: click "Check server", then "Test generation".

5. Paste or upload the job description and your CV.
   KEYWORD WORKBENCH (opens by default): the JD is on the left with keywords coloured
   green (in your CV), amber (partly) and red (missing); your CV text is on the right.
   Edit your CV, click "Refresh score", and repeat until you're happy (e.g. 75-80%).
   Then click "Create my resume from this text" to get Word + PDF, word for word.
   AI TAILORING (switch at the top of the page):
   Click "1 Analyze match only" to see what your CV proves and what is missing.
   If you genuinely have a missing skill, describe it in the gap box (it becomes evidence).
   Click "2 Tailor my resume + cover letter". The loop rewrites, fact-checks and re-scores.

6. Review the side-by-side view, edit if you want, and download the ZIP.
   Every run is also saved in the "outputs" folder next to this file.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- "No compatible llama.cpp model API": start llama-server first; or set LLAMA_PORT in start_windows.bat.
- "spent its whole output budget thinking": keep "Disable model thinking" ticked and start
  llama-server with --jinja (or --reasoning-budget 0).
- "prompt is larger than the context window": restart llama-server with --ctx-size 16384.
- PDF missing: needs Microsoft Word + pywin32 (pip install pywin32) or LibreOffice. Word files always work.
- Python not found: edit PYTHON=... at the top of start_windows.bat.
